📑 SmartBimPeï Suite — Technical Documentation & User Manual

Advanced Revit® Add-in Suite for Pre-Synthesis & Data Compliance Control

🌟 Introduction & Core Value Proposition

Who is this tool for?

This plugin was designed by and for BIM Managers, BIM Coordinators, Modelers (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Architecture), and Company Executives.

What is its main mission?

In the BIM world, clash detection and resolution (creating openings, communication) are time-consuming processes that are extremely costly for companies. SmartBimPeï eliminates these bottlenecks through:

  • Intelligent Automation: Forget creating openings manually one by one. This plugin handles complex network fusions and curved walls.
  • Standardized Communication: By leveraging OpenBIM standards (BCF), it facilitates communication with external teams on platforms like BIMcollab or BIM Track.
  • Management Visibility: A decision-making dashboard allows executives to evaluate the project's health at a single glance.

⚙️ Module 1: Smart Clash Detector (Geometric Synthesis Engine)

This module is the heart of SmartBimPeï. It doesn't just find clashes; it understands them to offer concrete solutions.

1.1 The Configuration Panel (Left side)

This panel allows you to configure your search with surgical precision.

  • Model A & Model B Selection:
    ⚙️ Usage: Choose the models to cross-check.
    💎 Added Value: Avoids analyzing the entire model unnecessarily.
  • Tolerance (mm):
    ⚙️ Usage: Define the acceptable penetration distance.
    💎 Added Value: Prevents "false positives".
  • "Min. Size" Filters:
    ⚙️ Usage: Enter a minimum dimension below which the element is ignored.
    💎 Added Value: Drastically reduces calculation time.
  • Auto-Reservation Margin:
    ⚙️ Usage: Indicates the clearance added around the duct.
    💎 Added Value: Anticipates insulation and fire stopping.

1.2 The Synthesis Table (Center)

  • Smart Grouping:
    ⚙️ What it does: Groups close clashes into a single ID.
    💎 Added Value: Reduces visual fatigue for the coordinator.
  • The "Resa Dimension" Column:
    ⚙️ What it does: Live display of the final hole dimensions.
    💎 Added Value: Architectural impact visible instantly.

1.3 Resolution Actions (Bottom)

👁️ "Isolate Clash" Button:

  • ⚙️ Usage: Opens a centered 3D view.
  • 💎 Added Value: Bright colors and neutral gray background for 1-second problem understanding.

🕳️ "Generate Auto-Reservation(s)" Button:

  • ⚙️ Usage: Places parametric "void" families with a single click.
  • 💎 Added Value: Manages Sliver Tolerance. What took 10 minutes now takes 1 millisecond.

1.4 Exports & Reports (Communication)

BCF Export (BIM Collaboration Format):

  • ⚙️ Usage: Generates a standard .bcfzip file.
  • 💎 Added Value: Imports into BIMcollab or Navisworks. No more endless email threads.

PDF & CSV Export:

  • ⚙️ Usage: Exports a visual report or Excel sheet.
  • 💎 Added Value: Perfect for coordination meetings.

💡 PRO TIP : Visualize the building context

When SmartBimPeï generates an isolation view (REVIT_CLASH_ISOLATION_VIEW), only the elements involved are visible. To understand exactly where you are in the building model, click on the "Glasses" icon at the bottom of the screen and select "Restore Temporary Hide/Isolate". The rest of the building will instantly reappear around your clash!
